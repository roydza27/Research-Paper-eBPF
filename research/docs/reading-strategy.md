# Research Reading Strategy

This document outlines the systematic reading methodology for digesting systems papers in the eBPF cloud security context.

## Three-Pass Reading Methodology
Adopted from S. Keshav's *"How to Read a Paper"*:

### 1. First Pass (Quick Scan - 5-10 minutes)
* **Goal:** Decide if the paper is worth reading and what category it belongs to.
* **Steps:**
  1. Read Title, Abstract, and Introduction.
  2. Scan Section headings.
  3. Read the Conclusion.
  4. Glance over references to note any familiar authors.

### 2. Second Pass (Grasp Content - 1 hour)
* **Goal:** Understand the primary contributions, architecture, and graphs, without proving mathematical or technical details.
* **Steps:**
  1. Inspect figures and diagrams closely.
  2. Read methodology and evaluation sections.
  3. Mark unread parts or terminology for lookups.

### 3. Third Pass (Deep Auditing - 2-3 hours)
* **Goal:** Virtually re-implement the paper. Question every assumption and find underlying flaws or unstated assumptions.
